Contact MyRetroTVs

Questions, corrections or feedback? We would like to hear from you.

Email: contact@myretrotvs.online

What to write to us about

  • Corrections to a date, fact or explanation in a guide.
  • Problems with the retro TV simulator on your device or browser.
  • Copyright or content concerns about anything on this site.
  • Ideas for future retro TV articles.

Helpful details to include

If you are reporting a problem, tell us the page address, your device and your browser so that we can reproduce it. We read every message, although we cannot always reply individually.

Before you write

Many common questions are answered in our FAQ and the blog. Please note that we cannot repair or value individual television sets.
