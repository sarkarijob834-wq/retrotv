Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyRetroTVs (“we”, “us”, “our”), available at myretrotvs.online, handles information when you visit the site.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you to submit personal information to use the site. The retro TV simulator runs entirely in your browser and does not send your interactions to us.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information in server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the date and time of the request. This data is used for security, troubleshooting and basic traffic measurement.

Cookies and similar technologies

At the time of writing, this site does not set its own tracking cookies. If we add analytics, advertising or other services in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Search and analytics services

We use Google Search Console to understand how our pages appear in search results. Search Console does not place tracking code on visitors' devices. Search engines and other third parties that you reach from our site may have their own privacy practices.

Email you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond and to improve the site, and we do not sell it.

External links

Our pages link to external websites such as reference sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies.

Children's privacy

The site is intended for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email contact@myretrotvs.online.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please use our contact page.
