Electrical and safety warning

Cathode-ray tube televisions and other vintage electronics can store a dangerous electrical charge even when unplugged, and they can be very heavy. Do not open the case of a television or attempt internal repairs unless you are properly trained. Use qualified technicians for servicing and follow local rules for recycling and disposal.
